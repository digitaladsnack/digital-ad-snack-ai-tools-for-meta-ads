# das-meta-ads-context 🧭

**The foundation skill.** Build your Meta ads brand brief once — every other Digital Ad Snack skill reads it first.

## What it does
Run this before anything else. It interviews you (or reads your product URL) and saves a `.agents/das-meta-ads-context.md` capturing:

- **Product snapshot** — what you sell, price, core benefit
- **Buyer persona(s)** — daily reality, trigger event, ranked pains, desires, objections
- **Voice-of-Customer language bank** — the *exact* words your customers use (marked `[REAL QUOTE]` vs `[INFERRED]`)
- **Positioning / USP**
- **Offer & proof**
- **8-Concept Fit Map** — which of the 8 static ad concepts fit you, and the angle for each

Then `das-ad-copy-generator`, `das-ad-hook-generator`, and `das-static-ad-scorer` all read this file, so your output is grounded in *your* audience — not generic AI guesses — and you never repeat yourself.

## How to use
Say *"set up my meta ads context"* or paste your product URL. Review the draft, correct it, and it saves automatically.
